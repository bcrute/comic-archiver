- **Collect extras for episodes you already saved.** On a site's dashboard card, open **Download: All types** (or the current selection), scroll to **Also save**, choose the extras and press **Save**. Press **Start** or **Continue** if downloading is paused. The app revisits recorded, already-saved episodes for missing selected extras before saving new episodes, keeping your existing CBZ/EPUB files and previously saved extras. Comic pages are not downloaded again, and completed extras are remembered across restarts.
- **Tapas extras:** creator's notes, background music, views, likes and comment counts, plus the comments themselves, newest first, up to 300 per episode.
- **Webtoon extras:** creator's notes, background music and likes. Saving views, comment counts or the comments themselves is not supported.
- **Tappytoon:** saving episode extras is not supported yet; comics and novels can still be saved as before.
- **All extras are optional.** Every one starts off, and each site's choices are kept separately. Only extras the provider supplies for an episode your account can still access are collected. Counts are a snapshot when collected, not live counters. Extras are stored under `.archive-state/extras` in the working library as JSON and separate sound files; they are not yet shown inside the app or included in cloud copies or exported CBZ/EPUB files. On Android they stay in the app's private working library. See [how to choose and save extras](https://comicarchiver.com/howto.html#episode-extras).
