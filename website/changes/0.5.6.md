- **Get episode lists before downloading.** On a provider’s Dashboard card, select **Download → Episode list only**, then press **Start** to fetch titles and availability. Open Library to choose which comics to save.
- **Choose individual episodes on desktop and Android.** Expand a series to choose which episodes to save. Checking or unchecking the whole series replaces those individual choices. The table header checkbox applies to all providers. A dash shows a mixed selection. Existing files stay where they are.
- **Keep your place in Library.** Refresh reopens the providers and series you were browsing and returns to your position when the row is still present. On desktop, hover over a title to read it in full.
