- **Get episode lists before downloading.** On desktop, choose **Get episode lists** in Library to fetch titles and availability from all signed-in providers that are ready. The Library shows progress for each provider. Start or continue downloads from the Dashboard when you’re ready.
- **Choose individual episodes on desktop.** Expand a series to choose which episodes to save. Checking or unchecking the whole series replaces those individual choices. The table header checkbox applies to all providers. A dash shows a mixed selection. Existing files stay where they are.
- **Keep your place in Library.** Refresh reopens the providers and series you were browsing and returns to your position when the row is still present. Hover over a title to read it in full.
