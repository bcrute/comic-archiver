- **Get episode lists before downloading.** On a provider’s Dashboard card, select **Download → Episode list only**, then press **Start** to fetch titles and availability. Open Library to choose which comics to save.
- **Choose individual episodes on desktop and Android.** Expand a series to choose which episodes to save. Checking or unchecking the whole series replaces those individual choices. The table header checkbox applies to all providers. A dash shows a mixed selection. Existing files stay where they are.
- **Keep your place in Library.** Refresh reopens the providers and series you were browsing and returns to your position when the row is still present. On desktop, hover over a title to read it in full.
- **One unreadable episode no longer stops a download.** An episode whose pages cannot be read is marked as not saved and the download moves on to the rest. It is tried again at the very end, after every other episode and list, up to three times. Anything still missing is shown as **Finished; some episodes could not be saved**, and starting the download again gives it new tries.
- **Images that fail to load are retried by themselves.** A single image that will not load no longer holds up its series. Its episode waits a little longer before each new try, the Dashboard shows the wait, and after five tries the episode is set aside as not saved.
- **Backblaze uploads recover from interruptions.** A busy answer, a failed answer or a dropped connection is tried again. An upload whose confirmation was lost is checked before anything is sent twice, and a sign-in that saved your key but could not be finished now says so.
- **Libraries with a damaged cache index open again.** The index is set aside and rebuilt. A series folder you renamed by hand is found again and its saved files are kept.
- **Windows frees cached pages again.** Cached originals are removed once the saved episode is safely on disk, so the cache no longer keeps growing after 0.5.5.
- **The Dashboard tells the truth when something goes wrong.** A stopped downloader, a missing library folder and a full disk are shown as they are, and free space is no longer reported as too small when new episodes go straight to Backblaze. A failed update check now says it failed.
- **Steadier Android downloads.** The saving notification ends with the last download, the phone stays awake while saving, turning the phone during sign-in keeps your place, and an import skips hidden files.
- **Smaller Mac download.** The Mac package leaves out the parts of Qt the app never uses.
