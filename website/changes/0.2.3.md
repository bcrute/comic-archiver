- **Nothing downloads until you press Go.** Opening Comic Archiver could carry on saving episodes from an earlier download by itself. It now waits for **Go**, and Go picks up where the download left off.
- **The window shows a download that is still saving.** When a download stopped on a problem, episodes could keep saving while the window showed it as stopped. The window now shows it as working until it has nothing left to save, and **Pause** stops it.
- **Pause stops a download left by an older version.**
