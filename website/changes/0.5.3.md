date: 2026-11-17
- **One odd series no longer stops the rest.** When a site gives an answer about one series that can't be read, that series is set aside and every other series is still checked and saved. The card lists the set-aside series with the reason, and they are checked again after the rest. A sign-in that has run out, a lost connection or a site asking the app to slow down still pauses everything as before, and so does the same failure in three series in a row.
- **Creator's notes, music, views and comments can be saved too.** "What to download" now has an "Also save" section: the note the creator writes under each episode, the episode's background music, its views and likes, and on Tapas the readers' comments (newest first, up to 300 per episode). Every one starts off, and only what the site shows readers of that episode is saved. They are kept for episodes saved from now on, in the library folder under `.archive-state/extras`, and are not uploaded or exported yet.
- **Tapas purchase history no longer stops the download.** Tapas only sends purchase-history pages in order within one visit. When the app restarted partway through the history, it asked for the next page straight away, Tapas refused it, and the download stopped with a wrong message about disk space. The app now reads the earlier pages again in order first. If Tapas still refuses a page, the history check is set aside for that round and the lists and series are still checked and saved. A download that stops for a reason the app doesn't recognize now says to send the log from Support if it happens again.
- **The space and time estimates stay on screen.** During a first download, "Est. space needed" and "Est. time left" went back to "Estimating…" each time one series was saved and the next was being read. They now keep showing the estimate until the download is done.
- **Changed choices are saved after the phone app was closed mid-download.** If the app was closed while it was saving an episode, changing the episode types or extras afterwards seemed to work, but after about 30 seconds nothing had been saved. The change is now saved straight away.
- **The Roadmap keeps itself up to date.** Work that ships in a release leaves the Roadmap when that release comes out, since the Changelog then lists it.
