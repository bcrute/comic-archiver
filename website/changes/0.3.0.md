- **Every site downloads at once.** Signed in to more than one of Tapas, Tappytoon and Webtoon? Each one now downloads alongside the others. **Start all** and **Pause all** start and pause them together.
- **A dashboard you can read at a glance.** One card across the top counts everything saved from every site, beside where it is kept. Then each site has a card of its own, with what it is doing in a few short lines and one button for what can be done now: **Sign in**, **Start**, **Continue** or **Pause**.
- **A download goes on by itself after a wait.** The app now keeps one record of your download instead of three that could disagree, which is what made a download pause for no clear reason. A dropped connection is tried again after 1, 2, 4, 8 and 15 minutes, then every 15 minutes until it is back.
- **Episodes the app can't save yet no longer stop a download.** It now finishes, and its card says how many there are. **Settings → Downloads** lists them by reason.
- **More control over download speed.** Medium aims for about half the rate of Fast, and Slow for about a quarter. Webtoon and Tappytoon images save faster on Fast, and each card shows the measured rate.
- **When a download stops, the window says why.** Each stop comes with what to do about it, such as signing in again. Press **Continue** once you have.
- **Pause stops the download, and Continue carries on from there.** Closing the window or switching off the computer pauses it the same way.
- **Asking for help is easier.** **Settings → About → Copy details for support** copies a short summary of each site's download. It has no series titles, folder names or sign-in details. **Save log for support** puts the app's log in one file in your Downloads folder.
- **Existing libraries are organized automatically.** On startup, the app moves older Tapas folders into `Tapas` beside `Tappytoon` and `Webtoon` in your library folder. **Open library folder** opens that folder in your usual file manager.
- **More reliable image recovery.** Valid images with extra trailing data are kept, and episodes blocked by the older image check are retried automatically.
- **Private sign-ins in copied Windows libraries.** App state in a library copied from elsewhere gets the same restricted access as new state. A Windows path-length failure while recording cloud backups is also fixed.
