- **Getting help is one button.** Settings has a new Support page with one button, **Email support**. It saves the app's log in your Downloads folder, shows it to you and opens a new email to me to attach it to. The log has no series titles, folder names or sign-in details. It replaces Copy details for support and Save log for support.
- **A Tapas error in your purchase history no longer stops a download.** When Tapas answered a page of your purchase history with an error, the step stopped there. The app now reads your lists and series anyway and asks for that page again on the next step.
- **A card stopped by an expired sign-in says so.** After you sign in again, the card reads "The sign-in had expired" instead of saying nothing about why it stopped.
- **Signing in to a different account says what you can do.** The message now says you can choose a different library folder to save that account separately.
- **A new icon.** The download arrow is bolder and stands out from the book, so it is easier to see in the taskbar and title bar.
