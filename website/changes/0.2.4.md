- **Bought paid-only Tapas episodes are saved.** A paid-only Tapas episode was skipped without a message even after you bought it, so a download could stop short of the end of a series. It is saved now.
- **The window checks your sign-in when it opens.** A sign-in the site no longer accepts showed as connected until Go found it had expired. The window now shows **Sign-in needed** straight away.
- **Windows: setup closes an open Comic Archiver.** Running a new setup while the app was open could fail. Setup now closes it first; a download pauses until you press Go.
- **Linux: starting a new copy while the old one is open says so.** Before, it only brought the old window forward, which looked as if the update had done nothing.
- **Two series with one name keep their own folders.** A later one now gets its site number added; folders you already have keep their names.
- **Fixes.** A Backblaze upload left unfinished no longer fails every retry once the series changes. A starting download is no longer sometimes reported as stopped. Replacing a Backblaze sign-in removes the old key. Updated certificate roots.
