Roadmap

What works today, what's being worked on, and what's on the wishlist. This is the one place to check what's planned.

Works today

In the current version.

  • Tapas, Tappytoon and WebtoonThe comics and novels your account can read, saved as CBZ and EPUB files.Works
  • WindowsWindows 10 and 11, 64-bit.Works
  • Linuxx86_64.Works
  • MacIntel processor, macOS 13 or newer. An early version: not notarized by Apple, tested on macOS 15.Works
  • AndroidThe same app on a phone: the same dashboard, sites and files. Android 15 or newer. A beta: download it from this site on the phone.Beta
  • Email supportSettings → Support saves the app's log and opens a new email to me to send it with.Works

Being worked on

Started, and coming in a future version.

  • One series never stops the restA series the site answers oddly for is set aside, shown with the reason and tried again later, while everything else carries on.Next release
