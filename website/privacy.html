Your privacy

Comic Archiver doesn't track you, and your login only ever goes to the comic site.

No tracking of any kind

No analytics, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no ads. The app never contacts me or any server of mine. There isn't one.

Your password goes to the site

You type it into the comic site's own sign-in page, shown inside the app's window. The app never reads it and never saves it.

What the app keeps

The sign-in cookie the site hands back, kept on your computer and only ever sent to that same site. Signing out removes it; uninstalling removes every one.

Who the app talks to

The comic site you picked and the servers it keeps its images on. Backblaze too, only if you choose to back up to your own storage there.

You don't have to take my word for it: all of the app's code is there for anyone to read. This website has no analytics or tracking cookies. Its theme script remembers your light or dark choice in your browser; its platform script reads what the browser says about your device to highlight the matching download. Neither script sends that information anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub, like any web host, records visitors' IP addresses to keep it running; see GitHub's note on this.
