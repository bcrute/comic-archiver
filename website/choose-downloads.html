How to pick what to download

Choose the series or episodes you want to save, then start the download when you’re ready.

Desktop · Android

On desktop

  1. Get episode lists. Sign in on the Dashboard. On each provider’s card, open Download, select Episode list only, and press Start. The card shows progress as the app fetches titles and availability.
  2. Choose what to include. Open Library or use Choose comics in Library in the Download menu. Expand a provider to see its series, then expand a series to pick individual episodes. Checking a series includes all its episodes and replaces any individual choices for that series.
  3. Start saving. Go back to the Dashboard, switch to Episode files in the Download menu, and press Start or Continue. Start all starts every ready provider in its selected download mode.

Reading the header checkbox

The checkbox at the top of the Library applies to every provider in the working library. A check mark means everything is included and the action is Uncheck all. A dash means the selection is mixed. A dash or an empty box offers Check all. Newly discovered series follow the last choice you made with this checkbox.

Signed in to another provider? After signing in, select Episode list only on that provider’s card and press Start. Other list fetches can keep running. If the provider is downloading files, pause it on the Dashboard first. If a provider returns a partial list, you can choose from the episodes it found.

Clear a retrieved list. On a provider’s Dashboard card, choose Download and then Clear episode list. Library continues showing saved episodes and keeps your download choices. Get the list again to see unsaved episodes.

On Android

  1. In Downloads, open the signed-in provider’s Download menu. Choose Episode list only, then tap Start. You can fetch another provider’s list from its own card.
  2. When episodes are available, tap Choose comics in Library on that card, or open Library → Episodes. Expand a series to select individual episodes. The checkbox above the providers shows a dash for a mixed selection. Tap Check all to include everything, or Uncheck all to clear the selection.
  3. Return to Downloads, select Episode files, then tap Start or Continue. To hide a retrieved list, pause that provider and choose Clear episode list. Your saved files and choices are kept.

What happens to your files and choices

  • Unchecking a series or episode keeps its saved CBZ and EPUB files and extras.
  • Your choices are saved for each provider in the current working library. New libraries start with everything checked.
  • The episode types you enabled for that provider still apply, and your account must have access to each episode. Extras have their own settings.
  • Applying a choice briefly pauses a running download, then it continues with the new choices. Paused downloads stay paused until you start them.
