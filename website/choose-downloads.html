How to pick what to download

Choose the series or episodes you want to save, then start the download when you’re ready.

Desktop · Android

On desktop

  1. Get episode lists. Sign in to the providers you use on the Dashboard. Open Library and click Get episode lists. The app checks titles and availability from signed-in providers that are ready. It shows each provider’s progress while it builds the list. You start file downloads separately in step 3.
  2. Choose what to include. Expand a provider to see its series, then expand a series to pick individual episodes. Checking a series includes all its episodes and replaces any individual choices for that series.
  3. Start saving. Go back to the Dashboard and choose Start or Continue for the provider you want. Start all starts every ready provider with its saved choices.

Get a list for one provider

On that provider’s Dashboard card, open Download, select Episode list only, and press Start. The card shows the selected mode and the fetch progress. Once episodes are listed, Choose comics in Library opens that list. When you’re ready to save files, select Episode files and press Start again.

Reading the header checkbox

The checkbox at the top of the Library applies to every provider in the working library. A check mark means everything is included and the action is Uncheck all. A dash means the selection is mixed. A dash or an empty box offers Check all. Newly discovered series follow the last choice you made with this checkbox.

Signed in to another provider? Click Get episode lists again once that provider is ready. Other list fetches can keep running. If the provider is downloading files, pause it on the Dashboard first. If a provider returns a partial list, you can choose from the episodes it found.

Clear a retrieved list. On a provider’s Dashboard card, choose Download and then Clear episode list. Library continues showing saved episodes and keeps your download choices. Get the list again to see unsaved episodes.

On Android

  1. Open Library and tap Episodes.
  2. Expand a provider and use the checkboxes to choose whole series. Check all and Uncheck all apply to all providers in this working library.
  3. Open Downloads and tap Start or Continue when you’re ready to save your choices.

What happens to your files and choices

  • Unchecking a series or episode keeps its saved CBZ and EPUB files and extras.
  • Your choices are saved for each provider in the current working library. New libraries start with everything checked.
  • The episode types you enabled for that provider still apply, and your account must have access to each episode. Extras have their own settings.
  • Applying a choice briefly pauses a running download, then it continues with the new choices. Paused downloads stay paused until you start them.
