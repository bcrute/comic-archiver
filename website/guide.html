Features

Sign in to your comic sites, press Start, and keep your episodes as files you can open in a reader app.

Comics and novels

Comics are saved as CBZ files and novels as EPUB files, which open in most comic and e-book reader apps.

Tapas, Tappytoon and Webtoon

Each site has its own card. Sign in to the sites you use and they can all download together.

Picks up where it left off

Stop whenever you like, close the window or switch off the computer. Next time it carries on from there.

Only what you can access

It saves the episodes your account can read, whether free, bought or already unlocked. It never buys, claims or unlocks anything for you.

Your files, your reader

Open saved files in the reader app you choose. On a computer, files go into the Comic Archiver folder in your user folder; on Android, use the app's Library to open or save a copy.

Optional episode extras

Tapas: creator's notes, music, views, likes, comment counts and comments. Webtoon: notes, music and likes. Tappytoon extras are not supported yet. See how to choose and save extras, including how to collect them for already-saved episodes in version 0.5.4 or later.

Optional cloud copy

If you'd like a copy in the cloud as well, the app can save to your own Backblaze B2 storage.
