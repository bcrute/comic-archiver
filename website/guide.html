How it works

One program you install and press Start. No extensions to add, no phone, no setup files.

Comics and novels

Comics are saved as CBZ files and novels as EPUB files, which open in most comic and e-book reader apps.

Three sites at once

Tapas, Tappytoon and Webtoon each have their own card. Sign in to each and they all download together.

Picks up where it left off

Stop whenever you like, close the window or switch off the computer. Next time it carries on from there.

Only what's yours

It saves the episodes your account can read. It never buys, claims or unlocks anything for you.

Your folder, your files

Everything goes into a folder called Comic Archiver in your user folder, one folder per series.

Optional cloud copy

If you'd like a copy in the cloud as well, the app can save to your own Backblaze B2 storage.
