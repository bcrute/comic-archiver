Getting started

Step-by-step help for the things people do most.

Save your library

  1. On the dashboard, each site has its own card. Choose Sign in on your site's card and sign in with your email and password, on the site's own page.
  2. Press Start on that card. If you use more than one site, sign in to each: they all download at once, and Start all starts every one.
  3. Leave it running. Pause stops it, and Continue carries on from there.

Use the account that holds your library. You need an account with your comic site; you do not need a separate Comic Archiver account.

Google and Facebook sign-in may not work inside the app. If your comic site lets you add an email/password login to that same account, you can use it here. This option is not available for every account.

WEBTOON can treat social and email logins as separate accounts even with the same email address. Use your original sign-up method to locate your library; creating another account will not bring over your purchases. See WEBTOON's account help.

If your account has no usable email/password login, contact support. Social sign-in is a current limitation.

Choose which comics to download

Available in 0.5.5. Open Library; on Android, choose its Episodes view. Expand a provider and check or uncheck each comic. Check all and Uncheck all at the top apply to every provider in this working library. Everything starts checked, including when updating an older installation.

To save just a few comics, choose Uncheck all, then check the ones you want. New discoveries follow that same default: they stay unchecked after Uncheck all, or checked after Check all. Choices are kept separately for each provider and working library.

Unchecking does not delete saved files. Existing CBZ/EPUB files and extras stay where they are. You can remove them yourself. Episode-type and extras settings still apply within checked comics.

The list shows comics the app has discovered so far. A new download round reads your account lists even with every comic unchecked, without saving unchecked comics. Use Refresh in Library to see newly discovered titles, choose your comics, then press Continue. A running download pauses while a choice is applied and then continues; a paused one stays paused.

Save notes, music and other extras

Extras are optional and every one starts off. Each site offers a different set:

  • Tapas: creator's notes, background music, views, likes and comment counts, plus the readers' comments themselves: newest first, up to 300 per episode.
  • Webtoon: creator's notes, background music and likes. Saving views, comment counts or the comments themselves is not supported.
  • Tappytoon: comics and novels can be saved, but saving episode extras is not supported yet.
  1. On the dashboard, choose Download: All types (or the current selection) on the site's card.
  2. Scroll to Also save, choose the extras you want and press Save. Each site's choices are kept separately.
  3. Press Start or Continue if downloading is paused. If it is already running, it pauses to apply your choices and then continues.

Only extras the site supplies for an episode your account can still access can be saved. Not every episode has a note or music. Counts are a snapshot from the time they are collected, not live counters. Saving comments takes longer because it needs extra requests to Tapas.

Already saved the episodes? Revisiting saved episodes requires version 0.5.4 or later. After you select extras, the next run revisits episodes recorded as saved in your library and collects the missing selected extras before saving new episodes. It keeps your existing comic and novel files, without downloading their pages again. Previously collected extras are kept, and restarting does not fetch completed extras again.

Version 0.5.3 collects extras only when an episode is first saved.

Where the extras go

They are stored separately from the comic or novel files, in the working library's .archive-state/extras folder, grouped by site and series. Notes, counts and comments are JSON files; music is saved as sound files alongside them.

Extras are not yet included in cloud copies or exported CBZ/EPUB files, or shown inside Comic Archiver. On Android they stay in the app's private working library; saving a copy of an episode does not include its extras.
