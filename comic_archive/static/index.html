Checking cloud connections…

Estimated Backblaze cost: free up to 10 GB, then about 2 cents a day, about 70 cents a month, for each 100 GB more?Cost details

Backblaze bills you itself, not through Comic Archiver. Comic Archiver never sees your payment details and is paid nothing.

  • Signing up is free and needs no card. The first 10 GB you store is always free.
  • Past 10 GB, Backblaze charges $6.95 a month for each 1,000 GB (1 TB) stored, and only for what is stored. Backblaze’s own pages count it by the day: 100 GB costs about 2 cents a day, about 63 cents a month. A large library of 12,000 episodes, about 240 GB, costs about 5 cents a day, about $1.60 a month.
  • Uploading is free. So is opening your episodes again, up to three times what you store, each month.
  • For more than 10 GB, add a card on Backblaze’s Billing page. Backblaze charges it once a month, for the month before.
  • You can set a limit. On Backblaze’s Caps & Alerts page, set the most you want to spend a day, a little above your library’s daily cost. Backblaze emails you as you near it and stops uploads when it is reached.
  • If Backblaze stops an upload, Comic Archiver tells you why and keeps the episodes on this computer.

One-time setup. Already have a Backblaze account? Start at step 2.

to use another bucket or key.

  1. Create your account.

    Create B2 account Sign in to Backblaze

    Sign up and verify your email. No card is needed.

    If your library is larger than 10 GB, add a card on the Billing page once you are signed in, and set the most you want to spend a day on Caps & Alerts.

    Open Billing Open Caps & Alerts

  2. Create a private library folder.

    Open Buckets

    Click Create a Bucket. A bucket is your cloud library folder.

    A bucket name can belong to only one Backblaze account, anywhere. A plain name like comics is almost certainly taken by someone else already. The name filled in above ends in letters and numbers made for your library, so it is unlikely to be taken.

    Type this name in Backblaze’s Bucket Name box, or choose your own: 6 to 63 letters, numbers or hyphens. If Backblaze says the name is taken, change it here and there so the two match. The app saves it when you connect.

    • Files in Bucket: Choose Private. Your files can only be opened by you and apps you give access to.
    • Object Lock: Choose Disable. This lets the archiver remove temporary uploads after testing the connection.

    Leave the other settings at their defaults, then click Create a Bucket.

  3. Create your library access key.

    Open Application Keys

    If that opens another page, find Application Keys in Backblaze’s left menu, under B2 Cloud Storage.

    Click Add a New Application Key. Fill in the form like this:

    • Name of Key: type comic-archiver. Backblaze allows only letters, numbers and hyphens here, no spaces.
    • Allow access to Bucket(s): choose your library bucket, the one from step 2. Not All.
    • Allow List All Bucket Names: leave it unticked.
    • Type of Access: choose Read and Write.
    • File name prefix and Duration: leave both blank.

    Then click Create New Key. Backblaze shows three values: keyID, keyName and applicationKey. Step 4 needs keyID and applicationKey.

    Backblaze shows the applicationKey only once. Keep that page open until step 4 is done. If it is closed too soon, create another key the same way; the old one can be deleted.

  4. Paste the key and connect.

    Copy the two values Backblaze just displayed. They stay on this device. You won’t need to enter them again.

  5. Check the connection.

    The test uploads two saved episodes, verifies them and deletes its cloud copies. Your local originals stay here.

  6. Choose where to save.

    Where new episodes go is chosen in one place: the Storage tab of these settings. Once the check passes, Backblaze is offered there.

Your Backblaze pages

Each opens in your browser, after Backblaze’s sign-in if needed.

  • Browse Files: see your saved episodes and download any of them. They are ordinary CBZ files that any comic reader opens, with or without Comic Archiver.
  • Reports: how much you store and what it costs, day by day.
  • Billing: your card and your monthly bills.
  • Caps & Alerts: the most you allow a day, and Backblaze’s emails as you near it.
  • Application Keys: delete Comic Archiver’s key here to take away its access.
Only episodes your account can already read, free or bought, are saved. Nothing is bought for you.
