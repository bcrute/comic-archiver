Comic Archiver

To start it, double-click "Comic Archiver" in this folder. If you used the
installer instead, Comic Archiver is in your Start menu. The first start can
take up to a minute; its window then appears by itself.

Windows may ask whether you want to run it, because the app is not signed
yet. Choose "More info" and then "Run anyway". If Windows says Smart App
Control blocked it and there is no "Run anyway", Comic Archiver can't be
used on this PC yet.

The first time:
1. Choose your site in the Site list.
2. Choose Sign in, and sign in on the site's own page in the window with
   your email and password. If you normally use Google or Facebook there,
   set a password on the site first.
3. Press Go.

Your saved episodes are in the folder "Comic Archiver" in your user folder
(C:\Users\<your name>\Comic Archiver): Tapas in "Library", Tappytoon in
"Tappytoon\Library". Comics are CBZ files and novels are EPUB files; they
open in comic and e-book reader apps. Uninstalling Comic Archiver removes
only the app: your saved episodes and your sign-in are kept.
