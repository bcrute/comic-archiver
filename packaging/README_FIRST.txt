Comic Archiver

To start it, double-click the file named "comic-archiver" in this folder and
choose "Run". If your file manager shows it as text instead, right-click it
and choose "Run as a program" (or allow executing it in its Properties).
After the first start Comic Archiver also appears in your applications menu.

Your saved library is the folder "Comic Archiver" in your home folder.

To update, close Comic Archiver, then unpack the new version and start it.
It offers to delete this older copy, and you stay signed in. Don't uninstall
first: that signs you out.

To uninstall, right-click Comic Archiver in your applications menu and
choose "Uninstall". That signs it out of every site and removes your
Backblaze key from this computer; your saved episodes are kept. Then
delete this folder.
